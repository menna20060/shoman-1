THEATER SOLUTIONS

From the stage to the last seat

Six disciplines, one contract. Each is planned with the others so sound, light and structure agree.

OUR APPROACH

Performance spaces that come alive

Theater architecture is unlike any other. Tell us your concept and our team turns it into reality with an architectural perspective of your venue, using imported panels and materials for walls and ceilings that give a strong professional finish.

HOW WE WORK

Design, build, test, deliver

01 · Design

Everything starts with an idea. We turn your ideas and style into a living space, advise on planning and design decisions, and prepare engineering drawings that make the most of every area.

02 · Construction

We are general contractors and skilled construction managers. We anticipate problems before they happen and cover most theater items: wall cladding, acoustic insulation, ceilings, floors, curtains, seating and the stage.

03 · Testing

We make sure all stage equipment is safe and in good working order, using engineering measuring tools for lighting systems, hoists and machinery, and measurement calculators for sound.

04 · Delivery

Because we support every aspect of the theater professionally, you receive a great space with the latest lighting and audio-visual equipment, with furniture and décor integrated with your logos and branding.
