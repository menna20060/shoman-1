THE SHOMAN STORY

Expertise in every detail

shoman was founded in February 2009 by a group of audio-visual experts. We design, supply and install auditorium seating, telescopic stands and complete theater systems.

From a hub in New Cairo and a second in Riyadh, one team handles the room from the acoustic plan to the final seat.
